I am an undergraduate student in Cyberspace Security at Tianjin University. My current interests lie in trustworthy AI, LLM safety, personalized alignment, high-performance AI systems, and secure software engineering.

My recent work focuses on safety and trustworthiness for large language models in multi-turn and personalized interaction scenarios. I have also worked on inference performance analysis for text-to-video generation models and engineering-oriented LLM deployment topics.

I have received awards in Huawei ICT Competition, mathematical modeling, software innovation, Kaggle/NeurIPS Lux AI, and university mathematics competitions. I enjoy building systems that connect rigorous reasoning with practical engineering.
