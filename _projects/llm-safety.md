This project studies safety and trustworthiness for large language models in multi-turn and personalized scenarios. The work explores dynamic safety boundaries, personalized jailbreak defense, preference data synthesis, and evaluation protocols for risk-aware model behavior.

Key directions include risk-context-aware GRPO, progressive-risk dialogue construction, automatic preference pair generation, and multi-dimensional safety evaluation.
