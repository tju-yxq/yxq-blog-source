This work analyzes inference bottlenecks in video generation models including Wan 2.2, HunyuanVideo, InfinityStar, CogVideoX, and Step-Video.

The project focuses on source-level model analysis, latency decomposition, memory bandwidth modeling, and practical optimization opportunities for large-scale generative AI deployment.
